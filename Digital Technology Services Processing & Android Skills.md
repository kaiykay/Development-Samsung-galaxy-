## Copper (Cu) - A Fundamental Element

Copper (Cu) has an atomic number of 29, meaning it has 29 protons in its nucleus. This metal is widely used in various industries due to its outstanding properties.

### Key Properties of Copper:
- **Electrical Conductivity:** Copper is one of the best conductors of electricity, making it ideal for wiring and electrical components.
- **Thermal Conductivity:** Copper is also highly efficient in transferring heat, used in heat exchangers and cooling systems.
- **Common Oxidation States:** Copper can exist in +1 and +2 oxidation states, making it versatile in chemical reactions and applications.
